# Satirical Parliament Video — Asset and Production Plan

## Creative target
Create a fictional political-satire video staged inside a recognizable, reference-led Moroccan parliamentary chamber. The point is to comment on public conduct and institutional patterns—grandstanding, empty promises, distraction, dozing, paper shuffling, and performative disagreement—not to fabricate evidence about a named person.

## Asset inventory
- Environment: `parliament_domed_chamber.glb` and optional `parliament_exterior.glb` from the architecture workflow.
- Fictional cast: six anonymous character archetypes generated by `tools/blender/generate_parliament_satire_characters.py`.
- Props to add in the next pass: desk nameplates without real names, papers, folders, microphones, phones, water glasses, clocks, voting buttons, and a podium.
- Animation set: idle/listen, talk with hand gestures, interrupt, glance at phone, doze/head-nod, shuffle papers, shrug, applaud sarcastically, and group reaction.
- Cinematic assets: close-up, speaker medium shot, wide chamber shot, reaction shot, empty-seat insert, and establishing exterior shot.

## Current character generator status
The first pass exports six separate GLBs and one preview PNG per character, with a manifest. Body/head/arm/hand/face pieces are separate named objects to make a later rigging pass easier. The models are not armature-rigged and should not yet be treated as animation-ready production characters. The workflow's structural checks only confirm basic file integrity, node/material counts, and preview image presence/dimensions.

## Acceptance gates
1. Run the architecture and character workflows successfully.
2. Inspect every rendered preview at full size; reject clipped faces, awkward silhouettes, hidden props, intersecting limbs, and poor framing.
3. Import the chamber and at least two characters into the intended renderer/engine. Check scale, orientation, materials, transparency, shadows, and contact with the floor/seats.
4. Add armatures and test facial readability and limb deformation before producing animation clips.
5. Stage a 10–20 second animatic with wide, medium, and reaction shots; evaluate whether the joke reads without relying on dialogue alone.
6. Do not label the pack finished until the rendered test scene and animatic have been visually reviewed.

## Tone and representation
Use invented names or no names. Do not copy a real politician's face, voice, party logo, or personal details without a deliberate rights/accuracy review. Keep fabricated lines and actions visibly within a fictional satire, not presented as documentary footage.
